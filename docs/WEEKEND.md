# Testing this over the weekend

A guide for using v1.9 on your phone for two days and coming back with
something useful. Not a checklist to complete - a plan for noticing.

It should take about ten minutes of deliberate poking on Saturday morning and
nothing at all after that. The rest is just using it.

---

## Before you start

Open it on the phone and add it to the home screen (Share, then Add to Home
Screen). It works in a tab too, but the installed one is the real thing: full
screen, no browser chrome, and it keeps working with no signal.

If you sync, check Settings → Sync is on and has pulled once. If you do not,
skip it - nothing below needs it.

**One thing worth knowing up front:** everything lives in that browser. Before
you start, Settings → General → Export backup, and put the file somewhere. Not
because anything is expected to go wrong; because a backup you did not take is
the one you wanted.

---

## How to report anything

Do not write it down anywhere else. Do not try to remember it.

The moment you notice something - it looks wrong, it did something odd, a word
grated, a button was hard to hit - press the pencil button at the bottom right
and type it starting with `#bug`:

```
#bug the week arrows are too close to the edge on my thumb
```

That is the whole process. It saves as you type; there is no save button and
no way to lose it. `#bug` is what makes it findable later.

Two other things the same box does, worth knowing because you will want them:

- Anything not starting with `#bug` is just a note. A number somebody says on
  the phone, a thought at a red light.
- A line starting with `!` goes to your inbox as something to do instead:
  `!book the dentist`. There is a Note/Task marker beside the box that shows
  which it will be before you press enter.

## Saturday morning: ten minutes

Do these in order. If any of them is annoying, that is the finding - write it
down and move on. Do not work around it.

**1. Add three things without typing a number.**
Type a title, press enter. That is it. The time control on the left and the
length on the right already hold an answer, so the task lands somewhere real.
Then push the little arrows to move one of them.

*Watch for:* is the time it picks sensible? Are the arrows big enough for your
thumb?

**2. Type one the long way.** `14:00 Call mom 45min`. Watch the two controls
change as you type.

*Watch for:* did they agree with what you meant?

**3. Put something in the backlog.** Tap Backlog on the toggle above the box,
type something you will do eventually, press enter. Then open the Backlog fold
at the bottom of the list and put one onto today.

*Watch for:* does it land somewhere sensible? Does the list feel like a place
you would come back to, or a place things go to die?

**4. Open the Library.** There are nine books in it, in the order you said.
Tap the top one - it opens a panel. Change the page you are on. Try switching
one to chapters and back.

*Watch for:* with nine books on one list, can you find things quickly? Add a
second list if you want to see it under load. This is the thing v1.9 changed
most, and the thing most likely to still be wrong.

**5. Break the day on purpose.** Tap Replan under the date, then "Something
came up". Put in something an hour long.

*Watch for:* can you read the answer without scrolling? Do you believe it?

**6. Take the tour.** Settings → General → **Replay tour**. Nine steps, two
minutes, in a sandbox that is thrown away afterwards - your real plan is not
touched. (On a laptop there are two more ways in: the `?` key, and Ctrl-K
then "tour".)

*Watch for:* anywhere you did not know what to do within about three seconds.

---

## Then just use it

Saturday and Sunday, plan the day the way you normally would. The things
worth noticing while you do:

- **The evening.** Around half nine, or the moment you finish the last thing,
  a card appears saying how the day went and offering to close it. That card
  is new and its whole point is the tone. If it ever reads like a report card,
  or makes you feel behind, that is the most important bug in this list.
- **The word "enough".** It should show up on days that deserve it and not
  feel like a participation trophy. If it feels like either, say which.
- **Anything that took two taps where one would do.**
- **Anything you looked for and could not find.**

---

## Sunday evening: send the bugs

1. Press the pencil, or the backtick key on a laptop.
2. Tap the `#bug` chip to filter down to just those.
3. Press **Export bugs**. It copies them all as a markdown list.
4. Paste that wherever we are talking.

If the clipboard refuses - some phone browsers do - select the notes and copy
them by hand. The button says so if it happens.

---

## What is new in v1.9, in one line each

So you know what you are looking at, and what is worth being suspicious of:

- **Quick-add** is three parts now: a time, the words, a length. All three are
  answered before you type.
- **Backlog**: a fourth shelf, under the inbox, for decided things with no day.
- **Library**: rebuilt. Lists fold, one item per list is loud, books can be
  counted in pages, films and series have their own shapes, and your reading
  plan is already in it.
- **Scratch**: `!` sends a line to the inbox as a task.
- **The tour**: reachable from `?` and the command palette, and no step can
  trap you any more.
- **Evening close**: the day gets an ending.

---

## If something goes badly wrong

Nothing here can lose your data quietly - every destructive action needs a
second tap, and there is a week of daily snapshots in Settings → General →
**Restore from a snapshot**. But if the app will not load at all:

1. Do not clear the site data. That is the one thing that actually loses it.
2. Take a photo of whatever the screen says.
3. `#bug` it from another device if you can, or just tell me.

# The shift of 2026-09-25

The owner worked a twelve-hour shift; this is what was done, what was
found, and what is left, with the proof beside each. Every claim here has a
test or a gate behind it, and the last sections are the ten minutes only the
owner can check, and the commits.

The brief had eight stages, stages 1 to 4 required. All eight are done. One
thing ran in another order than the brief gave it: the reading block's list
in the templates file was asked for after stage 3, and was done straight
after stage 1, because the owner's file had started carrying that field
during the day - and with it in the file, no commit could pass the gate
that reads the real file until the app read it too.

## What is new

- **The templates file pasted again** (v2.42, DECISIONS "A templates file
  pasted again brings today and the dates ahead along", TEMPLATE-JSON
  section 6). A date behind today is left as it was lived. Today and the
  dates ahead follow the file wherever they still hold what it gave them: a
  changed field arrives, a block taken away goes, a block added arrives.
  Nothing done by hand is undone - a ticked block, one moved by hand, a note
  written on the day, a task written by hand. **Today is cut at now**, as the
  brief recommended: a block that has ended stays as it was lived, ticked or
  not; one still running or ahead follows the file; a block the file adds
  arrives only where it has not ended. The preview says it: "3 dates ahead
  refreshed, 2 past dates untouched, 1 ticked block kept."
- **The archive on GitHub** (v2.43, docs/ARCHIVE-FORMAT.md, DECISIONS "An
  archive of every lived day, beside the backup", SYNC.md "The archive
  beside them"). In the backup's repo, through its token, nothing to set up:
  `archive/days/YYYY/MM/YYYY-MM-DD.json` for every lived day - its kind, its
  blocks, what was ticked and when, the meals with recipe, kcal and protein,
  the notes, the routines, the score - written when the day is over and
  again only when it changes afterwards; `archive/weekly/YYYY-MM-DD.json`,
  the whole plan once a week under its Monday, never written over. Nothing
  is ever deleted. Settings, Backup: "Archived until September 24." and
  Archive now, with what went wrong said on the same line. When a block was
  ticked is kept now (`Task.doneAt`), so the archive can say it.
- **A reading block names its Library list** in the file (v2.41): by name,
  whatever the case; one the Library has not got yet waits for it; the
  preview says "Read reads from Main: A first book".
- **A tick is never lost to a change of kind** (DECISIONS), and **two tabs
  take in what the other saved**, an erase included (DECISIONS).

## What was checked, and held

Each of these is a test now, and none needed a change.

- **The owner's month** (`ownersMonth.test.ts`, the real file, six weeks of
  two days, two nights and four free days from 30 September, across the
  night the clocks go back): every kind where it belongs - the first night,
  the second, the day after nights, the free days; every routine at its
  time on its weekday, none needing one or refused, none on a Sunday; each
  night's hours on the morning after once, nothing twice on any date; each
  meal block walking its recipes a date at a time and round again.
- **The nights a clock can break** (`nightClock.test.ts`): a shift from 19:00
  to 07:00 on the night the clocks go back is thirteen hours and on the
  night they go forward eleven; it ends by itself at 07:00 and not a minute
  before; the snack at 02:30 and the journey home at 07:15 and 07:40 are the
  morning's; nothing stands in a sleep; the last nights of October and of
  December put their hours on the first of the next month and year, and the
  archive files them there.
- **The app opened at 02:30 in a night shift** (`e2e/night-open.e2e.ts`, both
  sizes): today is the night's morning - the snack running with its minutes
  left, the journey home, the day after nights - not an empty date.
- **A phone with no network in a shift** (`e2e/offline-shift.e2e.ts`): a
  block ticked offline goes up when the network is back, and the archive
  writes the day with the tick and its moment the next morning.
- **The three pastes** (`pastedAgain.test.ts`): Kitchen's recipes, the
  Library's shelf and North's text each say in the preview what Save makes,
  the second paste changes nothing, one undo puts everything back.
- **The hunt** (`e2e/hunt.e2e.ts`, `archive.test.ts`, `kitchen.test.ts`): a
  sheet open while another tab imports a file that takes its task away
  closes by itself; a 300-letter title pushes nothing sideways; a double
  press on Apply or on Add a block does one; a recipe with no kcal shows the
  numbers it has, or none, on the meal and in the archive; the archive's
  first run into a repo with nothing of it yet writes the lived days, oldest
  first, and a token taken back is said on the line, naming it, with nothing
  marked done.
  A template with no blocks was the one case that did not hold - row 8
  above.

**And what it got wrong and did not catch in time: the report was late.**

keyboard pass and nothing pushed, so none of this reached the app before the
evening of the 25th. The keyboard pass had failed three runs before that,
none of them on the app: once the machine was asleep

commit while the pass was still walking it and the page reloaded under it,
and once it read a file halfway through an edit. Two runs had also
overlapped, one measuring while the next built. None of those runs is
counted: the gates named above are the final run, alone, on the final tree.
